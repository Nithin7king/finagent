INDIAN INCOME TAX RULES — FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Finance Act 2024

=== NEW TAX REGIME (DEFAULT) ===

Income Slabs and Tax Rates (New Regime):
- Up to ₹3,00,000: NIL
- ₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000: 5%
- ₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000: 10%
- ₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000: 15%
- ₹12,00,001 – ₹15,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹15,00,000: 30%

Rebate under Section 87A: If total income does not exceed ₹7,00,000, tax payable is NIL under new regime.
Standard Deduction (New Regime): ₹75,000 for salaried individuals (Budget 2024 enhancement).

=== OLD TAX REGIME (OPTIONAL) ===

Income Slabs and Tax Rates (Old Regime):
- Up to ₹2,50,000: NIL
- ₹2,50,001 – ₹5,00,000: 5%
- ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹10,00,000: 30%

Rebate under Section 87A (Old): If total income does not exceed ₹5,00,000, full tax rebate.

=== KEY DEDUCTIONS (OLD REGIME ONLY) ===

Section 80C — Investments & Payments (Max ₹1,50,000):
- EPF (Employee Provident Fund) contributions
- PPF (Public Provident Fund) — 15-year lock-in, 7.1% rate
- ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) — 3-year lock-in, market-linked returns
- NSC (National Savings Certificate)
- Life Insurance premiums
- Home loan principal repayment
- Tuition fees for children (max 2 children)
- Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana contributions

Section 80D — Health Insurance Premiums:
- Self + family: up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if senior citizen)
- Parents: up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if senior citizens)
- Preventive health check-up: up to ₹5,000 (within above limits)

Section 80CCD(1B) — NPS additional contribution: ₹50,000 (over and above 80C)
Section 24(b) — Home loan interest: up to ₹2,00,000 for self-occupied property
Section 80E — Education loan interest: full deduction (no limit), up to 8 years
Section 80G — Donations: 50%–100% depending on organization
Section 80TTA — Savings account interest: up to ₹10,000

=== HRA (HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE) ===

HRA exemption = MINIMUM of:
1. Actual HRA received
2. 50% of basic salary (metro cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata)
   OR 40% (non-metro)
3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of basic salary

=== CAPITAL GAINS TAX ===

Equity Mutual Funds / Stocks:
- Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG, held < 1 year): 15% (20% from July 2024 under Finance Act 2024)
- Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG, held > 1 year): 10% above ₹1,00,000 exemption (12.5% from July 2024)

Debt Mutual Funds (post March 2023):
- Taxed as per income tax slab (no LTCG benefit)

Real Estate:
- STCG (held < 2 years): taxed as per slab
- LTCG (held > 2 years): 20% with indexation (12.5% without indexation from Budget 2024)

=== ADVANCE TAX SCHEDULE ===

If total tax liability > ₹10,000 in a year:
- By June 15: 15% of tax
- By September 15: 45% of tax
- By December 15: 75% of tax
- By March 15: 100% of tax

=== FILING DEADLINES ===

ITR filing deadline: July 31 (non-audit cases)
ITR filing with audit: October 31
Belated return: December 31 (with late fee)
Late fee: ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if income < ₹5 lakh)
