Income Tax Slabs (New Regime) - General Reference
Under the new tax regime in India, income up to Rs 3,00,000 is not taxed. Income between
Rs 3,00,001 and Rs 6,00,000 is taxed at 5%. Income between Rs 6,00,001 and Rs 9,00,000 is
taxed at 10%. Income between Rs 9,00,001 and Rs 12,00,000 is taxed at 15%. Income between
Rs 12,00,001 and Rs 15,00,000 is taxed at 20%. Income above Rs 15,00,000 is taxed at 30%.
Note: tax slabs change with each budget cycle, so always verify against the latest official
government notification before relying on this for real filing decisions.

Section 80C Deductions
Under the old tax regime, Section 80C allows deductions up to Rs 1,50,000 per year for
investments in instruments such as Public Provident Fund (PPF), Employee Provident Fund
(EPF), Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds, National Savings Certificate (NSC),
and life insurance premiums.

Loan Refinancing Basics
Refinancing a loan generally makes financial sense when the new interest rate is at least
1 to 2 percentage points lower than your current rate, and you plan to hold the loan long
enough for the interest savings to outweigh any processing or foreclosure fees involved.
